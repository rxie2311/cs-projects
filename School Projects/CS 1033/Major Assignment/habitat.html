The Habitat of Pandas

Habitat Environment

Pandas are found exclusively within these regions in China:

  • Sichuan
  • Shaanxi
  • Gansu

The forests they live in are damp and misty, often in a more mountainous area. The average altitude that they reside in is 2400 - 3000 meters above sea level. The forests they live in are usually covered in a thick bamboo growth, suitable for their diet of mainly bamboo. However, due to human activity such as hunting and deforestation, the habitat of pandas has severely decreased over the years.
