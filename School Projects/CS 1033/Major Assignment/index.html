About the Panda

Origins, Appearance and Behavior

The panda is a part of the bear family. It is a species that is exclusively found in China, making it China’s national animal. Its fur is mainly white, with black spots around its eyes, and black fur around its legs and paws. Pandas are generally round and chubby, and on average weigh 70 - 120 kilograms as an adult. Pandas are very peaceful creatures, often spending their time sleeping, eating or relaxing. They mostly live on their own, choosing only to interact with others of their kind during mating season in the spring. A female panda will usually only care for one cub at a time, up to around 18 months before setting off independently. They are mostly non-confrontational, however, they will fight if they feel threatened. Due to the carefree nature of pandas, it is hard for them to survive and reproduce without human intervention.
