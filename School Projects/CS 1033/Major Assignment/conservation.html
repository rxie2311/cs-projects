The Conservation of Pandas

Conservation Efforts

Due to the declining population of pandas, there are efforts made within China to conserve as many of them as possible. The Chinese government has partnered with organizations such as WWF to create panda reserves within China, which contain wild pandas. These reserves are planned to have bamboo pathways that link different forests together to give the pandas more space to roam around and breed. Currently, there are about 40 panda reserves spread across southern China. Pandas are not only kept in reserves, but in zoos, so humans can help take care of them. All revenue generated by the pandas within zoos overseas must donate a portion of their earnings to the conservation effort of pandas in China. With all the conservation effort, pandas are finally no longer almost extinct, however their population is still low, making them still endangered.
