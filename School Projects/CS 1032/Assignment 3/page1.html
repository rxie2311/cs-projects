Meet ABC Solutions

Our Story

ABC Solutions was founded in 2004 by current CEOs, Barry Oliver and Joseph Maroon. Back then, they both shared a common dream, to create AI techology that would shock the world. ABC Solutions was created as a response to that dream. It strives to be innovative, creative and imaginative in the world of AI. This ensures that the CHATBOT, the signature AI tech of ABC Solutions is programmed creatively, while also staying ahead of competitors not only in efficiency, but in using resources such as computing power more wisely and effectively. All in all, ABC Solutions is a company with two passionate CEOs, as well as a passionate team, ready to put their creative minds into creating and innovating the future of AI for the good of humanity.
